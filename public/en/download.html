Frequently asked questions

Before you download

Is my device compatible?

If your iPhone runs iOS 17 or later, it is compatible. LiDAR is not required: it only adds the automatic room scan.

How much does it cost?

The app is free. The optional one-time "Acoustic Detector Complete" purchase (CA$4.99) unlocks Treatment and Calculators, with no subscription.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Measurements and calculations happen on the device, with no account or server. Only the purchase and restore go through the App Store.

Are the measurements accurate?

The iPhone's microphone gives an estimate, not a certified measurement. Optional manual calibration lets you sharpen the result.

Which languages?

Fully in English and French, with an EN / FR button in the main menu.

How do I get help?

Email us at j_david@live.ca or visit the Contact page.
