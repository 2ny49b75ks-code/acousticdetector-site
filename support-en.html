Frequently asked questions

Are the decibel readings calibrated?

Not by default. Acoustic Detector uses the iPhone's built-in microphone to estimate sound level — it's indicative, not a certified instrument. The Sound Meter mode offers optional manual calibration against a reference instrument to improve accuracy.

Does the LiDAR room scan work on every iPhone?

No, only on Pro models equipped with a LiDAR sensor (iPhone 12 Pro and later). On other iPhones, room dimensions are entered manually in the Room Treatment mode — every calculation feature remains available.

Is the reverberation (RT60) calculation exact?

The Room Treatment mode offers two estimates: a theoretical calculation (Sabine formula, based on your dimensions and materials) and a live measurement via a noise burst. Both are useful approximations for guiding an acoustic treatment, not laboratory measurements.

Are my measurements sent anywhere?

No. No data ever leaves your device. See our privacy policy.

Is the app available in French?

Yes. Acoustic Detector is fully bilingual — an FR/EN button in the main menu switches the interface language, independent of your iPhone's system language.

Is there a subscription?

No. Acoustic Detector is a one-time purchase on the App Store ($19.99 CAD), no subscription or in-app purchases. Updates are included.

Is the app available on Android?

Not at this time — Acoustic Detector is available on iPhone (iOS 17 or later).
